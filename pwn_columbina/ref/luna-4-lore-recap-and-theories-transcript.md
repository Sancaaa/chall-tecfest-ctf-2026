She wondered if all her work was for nothing, until Columbina finds her while looking for a way out and delivers Rerir’s message. Tholindis learns the truth, forgives Rerir, recognizes her own mistakes, and thanks Columbina for bringing the news and letting her research matter. I don’t mind that Rerir and Tholindis may never truly reunite for eternity. I just wish the execution gave their story the space it deserved.

But here’s one of Genshin’s biggest unanswered mysteries: we still don’t actually know who created Teyvat. We don’t know who made the planet in the absolute sense. In-game text strongly claim the Heavenly Principles made the world anew and built the Human Realm we call Teyvat after defeating the dragon Sovereigns, while Nibelung ruled the Old World with the Moon Sisters and dragons. But neither the Heavenly Principles nor Nibelung seem to be the first to create the planet based on what was revealed so far. Which means there is still room for a truth we haven’t even named yet.

Speaking of the truth… there’s one waiting on the Frost Moon. Traveler’s spaceship is currently on the Frost Moon. It’s the ship the twins boarded when they were traveling between worlds, before arriving on Teyvat. It originally landed in Nod-Krai, but has since been moved to the surface of the Frost Moon by the Heavenly Principles. Columbina tells the Traveler she suspects the ship was intentionally hidden. “Perhaps someone has certain truths they wish to hide from you, or from others.” Most likely the Heavenly Principles. The Traveler reads the flight logs and discovers something unsettling: the ship passed through Teyvat once. And continued. Until they were forced to crash-land at Nod-Krai. And from what their Sibling told them… they might not know either. Inside the ship, the Traveler retrieves a new outfit, and reading the lore on it was one of the first things I did, because I needed to know what the game thought was important enough to tie to the Traveler’s identity. And I was not prepared for the question it raised… the AI that piloted the Traveler and their Sibling’s spaceship… is the Heavenly Principles? The Heavenly Principles is an AI…
