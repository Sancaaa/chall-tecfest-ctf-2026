Nothing in the text definitively states that Saarelainen wasn’t human. In fact, he’s usually described as a noble “man.”

Alice even tells us directly that Dottore is attempting to transcend destiny, working toward an experiment that only a god could complete. So the question becomes unavoidable: what if Dottore isn’t just trying to become a god? What if he’s trying to become a Descender?

If Dottore truly gained full control over Life, Death, Time, Space, and Reason he’d be able to decide what can exist, how it exists, when it exists, where it exists, and why it exists at all. At that point, he’d be writing reality itself, Fate itself.

But during the quest, he fails. The reason he couldn’t hold onto those powers is because he lacked a valid authority or role that was recognized by the world itself. Just like Columbina, but on a much larger scale. And that’s where Irminsul comes in. Irminsul is the database that records identities, histories, roles, permissions, everything that determines what is allowed to exist and who has the right to wield what power. As long as Irminsul stands, it enforces those rules.

If Irminsul were to be… destroyed, the world might forget its own rules and permissions… Life, Death, Time, Space, and Reason would lose their bindings to specific roles or authorities. Teyvat would lose the ability to reject foreign authority. In that chaos, Dottore could insert himself as the new ruler of the world - maybe replace the Heavenly Principles entirely. Or a feat of this magnitude would earn him the title of Descender. Which is probably WHY he wants to burn down the Irminsul Tree.

And if Dottore really is attempting to become a Descender, that would make him the Fifth.

There’s an eerie real-world parallel here between doctors and the number 5. And I wouldn’t be surprised if the HoYo writers knew about this because of how much research they do. What if I told you the symbol many people associate with medicine - the Caduceus - was never actually a medical symbol? The true symbol of medicine should have been the Rod of Asclepius. The Caduceus belongs to Hermes, a god of travel and commerce but also of trickery, deception, boundaries, and miscommunication. Because of a mistake made in the early 1900s, American medical institutions ended up using Hermes’ symbol, and it never got corrected.

Hermes is associated with the planet Mercury, and in ancient numerology, Mercury is tied to the number 5. Fifth Descender.

Just as Hermes’ Caduceus was mistakenly seen as the universal symbol of medicine, Dottore alters memories and convinces entire civilizations to worship false gods and false moons. If Hoyoverse intended for there to be a “Fifth” Descender, it’s hard to imagine a better fit.

And there’s one last warning hidden in all of this. Genshin constantly reminds us that history repeats. The future reflects the past. The Great War of Vengeance was a war between Descenders… and it hasn’t repeated yet. Which means we’re overdue. And if Dottore truly becomes another Descender, then eventually, the Traveler (the Fourth Descender) will have to face him.

The more you look at how Teyvat works, the more it starts to resemble a computer system. I also think this analogy will help us better understand how Teyvat works for now. So excuse me while I brush the dust off that Computer Science degree. Cause we love women in STEM am I right? :’)

Teyvat is a computer system. At the very center of any computer system is something called a kernel. The kernel is the core of an operating system. It acts as the bridge between software and hardware, managing essential tasks like memory, processing time, and communication between different parts of the system. It makes sure programs don’t interfere with each other, enforces security, and handles critical requests, like saving data or running multiple tasks at once. In simple terms, the kernel is the brain of the operating system.

In Teyvat, Celestia fills that role. Celestia functions as the core system that decides how the world runs. And the Heavenly Principles acts like the root administrator, the highest possible level of access. In computer terms, this is often called UID 0, the very first (like First Descender) and most “powerful” user. Nothing can request changes from the kernel unless it has root permission. This is the reason why in software engineering, we had to be reaaaallly careful giving users root access. Likewise, only the Heavenly Principles are able to directly enforce or alter the fundamental rules of Teyvat.

The Four Shades operate like background programs, or daemons. These are processes that run continuously without direct interaction, quietly making sure everything functions as intended. Things like time, space, life, and death continue to operate because these background processes are always running, even when no one is aware of them.

Irminsul acts as the system’s database and file storage. It records names, identities, memories, history, permissions, essentially everything the world recognizes as real. It defines what exists, what doesn’t, and how things are allowed to behave. If something isn’t properly recorded in Irminsul, the system treats it as unstable. In this analogy, forbidden knowledge and Abyssal power function like malware, data that doesn’t belong in the system and causes corruption when introduced.

Before Celestia took control, the Three Moon Sisters functioned as core system processes as well. But when the Heavenly Principles replaced the old world order, those lunar processes were shut down and replaced by the Shades. The moon system became outdated, no longer compatible with the current version of Teyvat.

Columbina is tied to that older lunar system. She’s like a legacy process trying to run on an operating system that no longer supports her code. Because her authority comes from a deprecated source, the system rejects her. That rejection is what causes her existence to destabilize and be incompatible with Teyvat.

For Columbina to stop being rejected, her authority needs to be recognized again. In simple terms, the system needs to accept her as valid. That means Irminsul has to either rewrite how her existence is recorded or approve her under a new role that fits the current world order. And because Irminsul governs names and permissions, characters with deep knowledge of it could potentially make that possible. Nahida, the Wanderer, and members of the Hexenzirkel like Alice or Nicole all understand how to navigate Irminsul.
